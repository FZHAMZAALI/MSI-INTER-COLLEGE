Privacy Policy

Miyan Sahab Islamia Inter College, Bakshipur, Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh – 273001 · Effective 14 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Miyan Sahab Islamia Inter College ("the College", "we") collects through its website msi-inter-college.pages.dev and its online admission portal, why we collect it, and how it is protected. It is written in line with India's Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

a) When you sign in with Google

We receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We do not receive or store your Google password.

b) When you submit an admission application

  • Student details: name, date of birth, category, religion (optional), nationality, blood group (optional), Aadhaar number (optional), class and stream applied for, previous school and marks.
  • Parent / guardian details: names, occupation, annual income range, mobile number(s), email and address.
  • Uploaded documents: student photograph and, if provided, mark sheet, Aadhaar card, transfer certificate and birth certificate.

c) When you pay the registration fee

Online payments are processed by PayU, an RBI-authorised payment aggregator. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on PayU's secure pages and are never received or stored by the College. We keep only the transaction reference, amount, payment method and status.

d) When you use the contact form

Your name, mobile number, subject and message.

e) Technical information

Your browser stores your sign-in session and display preferences (text size, contrast) on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. Why we use it

  • To process admission applications, verify documents and communicate the result.
  • To receive fees and issue receipts.
  • To reply to enquiries and send updates about your application by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email.
  • To meet the requirements of the U.P. Board, the Government of Uttar Pradesh and other authorities, where the law requires.

We do not sell, rent or share your information for marketing.

3. Who can see it

  • College staff authorised by the Principal, through a secured admin panel that requires Google sign-in.
  • Service providers that run the system for us, only for that purpose: Cloudflare (hosting, database and file storage), Google (sign-in) and PayU (payments).
  • Government or education authorities when required by law.

4. How it is protected

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The database cannot be read directly from the internet; every request is checked on the server.
  • Uploaded documents are private and can be opened only by authorised staff through links that expire within an hour.
  • Access to the admin panel is limited to Google accounts approved by the College.

5. How long we keep it

Application records and documents are kept for the admission session and as long as needed for the student's records and legal or audit requirements. Applications that do not result in admission are deleted after the session ends, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

6. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information you have given us, or withdraw consent for its use (which may mean we cannot process the application). Contact the College office using the details below.

7. Children

Admission applications for students are to be filled by, or with the consent of, a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version and its effective date are always shown on this page.
