About the College

Rooted in heritage, built for tomorrow.

M.S.I. Inter College, located at Bakshipur in the heart of Gorakhpur, is a government-aided minority institution established in 1908. Recognised for High School in 1922 and Intermediate in 1938, it is affiliated with the U.P. Board of High School & Intermediate Education, Prayagraj.

मियाँ साहब इस्लामिया इण्टर कॉलेज हिन्दी एवं अंग्रेजी माध्यम में कक्षा 6 से 12 तक शिक्षा प्रदान करता है। परिसर में 40 कक्षाएं, अटल टिंकरिंग लैब, ICT लैब, विज्ञान प्रयोगशालाएं, विशाल खेल मैदान, सौर ऊर्जा एवं समृद्ध पुस्तकालय उपलब्ध है।

Admissions · Session 2027-28

Join the M.S.I. family.

Admission is open for Classes 6 to 12 for students who have passed the qualifying examination from a recognised school.कक्षा 6 से 12 तक प्रवेश उपलब्ध है।

01

Register online · ऑनलाइन पंजीकरण

Create an account, fill in the details and upload the student photo and documents. You get a Registration ID instantly.

02

Document verification · दस्तावेज़ सत्यापन

Visit the college office with the original documents and a printout of your registration slip.

03

Track your status · स्थिति देखें

Log in or use your Registration ID + mobile number to see whether your application is under review or approved.

04

Pay fee online · ऑनलाइन शुल्क

Pay securely by UPI, debit/credit card or net banking — or at the college office — and download your fee receipt.

How do I apply for admission? · प्रवेश कैसे लें?
ऑनलाइन पंजीकरण करें, Registration ID प्राप्त करें, फिर मूल दस्तावेज़ों के साथ कार्यालय आएं।Register online on this website to receive a Registration ID, then visit the office with your original documents for verification.
Which board is the college affiliated with? · बोर्ड मान्यता?
माध्यमिक शिक्षा परिषद, उत्तर प्रदेश (BHSIEUP), प्रयागराज।The college is affiliated with the U.P. Board of High School & Intermediate Education, Prayagraj.
Which streams are available in Class 11? · कौन से संकाय उपलब्ध हैं?
Science (PCM and PCB), Commerce and Arts / Humanities.
Is hostel accommodation available? · छात्रावास सुविधा?
हाँ, दूरदराज के छात्रों के लिए सुरक्षित एवं अनुशासित छात्रावास उपलब्ध है।Yes — a safe, disciplined hostel is available for outstation students. Select “Hostel required” in the registration form.
How can I check my application status? · आवेदन की स्थिति?
Log in to the student portal, or use “Track Application” with your Registration ID and the mobile number used in the form.
Who are the Principal and Manager? · प्रधानाचार्य एवं प्रबंधक?
Principal: Shri Zafar Ahmad Khan. Manager: Mahboob Sayeed “Haris”.
