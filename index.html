About the College

Rooted in heritage, built for tomorrow.

M.S.I. Inter College, located at Bakshipur in the heart of Gorakhpur, is a government-aided minority institution established in 1908. Recognised for High School in 1922 and Intermediate in 1938, it is affiliated with the U.P. Board of High School & Intermediate Education, Prayagraj.

Admissions · Session 2027-28

Join the M.S.I. family.

Admission is open for Classes 6 to 12 for students who have passed the qualifying examination from a recognised school.

01

Register online

Create an account, fill in the details and upload the student photo and documents. You get a Registration ID instantly.

02

Document verification

Visit the college office with the original documents and a printout of your registration slip.

03

Track your status

Log in or use your Registration ID + mobile number to see whether your application is under review or approved.

04

Pay fee online

Pay securely by UPI, debit/credit card or net banking — or at the college office — and download your fee receipt.

Required documents

Please keep these ready.

  • Previous class mark sheet
  • Transfer Certificate (T.C.)
  • Birth certificate
  • Aadhaar card copy
  • 4 passport-size photographs
  • Residence & caste certificate (if applicable)

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Call or WhatsApp the office.

Call Office WhatsApp
How do I apply for admission?
Sign in with your Google account, fill the online form, pay the fee online or at the office, and visit with original documents for verification.
Which board is the college affiliated with?
The college is affiliated with the U.P. Board of High School & Intermediate Education, Prayagraj.
Is this a boys' school?
Yes — admission is for boys, Classes 6 to 12.
Which streams are available in Class 11?
Science (PCM and PCB), Commerce and Arts / Humanities.
Is hostel accommodation available?
Yes — hostel facility is available for outstation students. Tick “Hostel” in the registration form.
How can I check my application status?
Log in to the student portal, or use “Track Application” with your Registration ID and the mobile number used in the form.
Who are the Principal and Manager?
Principal: Shri Mukhtar Ahmad. Manager: Mahboob Sayeed “Haris”.
